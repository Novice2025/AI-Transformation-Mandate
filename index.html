Why This Matters Now

The confluence of AI-driven job function elimination and an outdated education system is creating an unprecedented economic shift. Success hinges not on what you automate, but on the new soft skills you master. Every industry is poised for a massive transformation within the next five years. To navigate this, businesses must pivot their focus toward developing entrepreneurial capabilities, leveraging AI for scalable growth, and proactively reskilling their teams.

Build Your Edge, by Daby

This Executive Action Guide, developed as part of the Build Your Edge, by Daby framework, emphasizes that the future of work rewards the entrepreneur, regardless of their title. Invest in personal branding and the skills that AI cannot automate (pitching, visioning, and human connectivity) to thrive in the inevitable economic shifts.
